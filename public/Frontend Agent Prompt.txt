## BROWSE MODEL (CRITICAL)

### ID-based navigation (current)
- State: `path` or `pathIds` = `string[]` of **category UUIDs**
- Data: `categories:getBrowseTree()` → nested tree with `id`, `name`, `path`, `is_leaf`, counts
- Logic: `computeTreeBrowseState(stock, pathIds, catTree, search)` in **`categoryBrowse.js`**
- At **leaf**: show variants where `row.category_id === leafNode.id`
- Folders: children from tree; counts from stock under subtree

### Do NOT use for browse
- `computeBrowseState` in `hierarchyNav.js` (name-based) — **legacy**, pages migrated off it

### Still use hierarchyNav for
- `stockMatchesSearch(row, query)`
- `getDisplayBreadcrumb(row)` on variant cards
- `barcodeMatchesVariant(row, code)`

### Sidebar
`HierarchyTreeSidebar` — `catTree` only, `pathIds`, collapsed by default

## DATA RULES

- Categories/products use **UUID** `id` — never slug keys like `'school-uniforms'`
- Products: **leaf** `category_id` via `categories:getProductLeaves` or `CategoryPicker`
- School badge: `school_id` on product (admin), not Schools branch as product category
- Stock rows: `category_path`, `school_name`, `attributes`, `path_label` on products table
